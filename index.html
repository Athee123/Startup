This is a design preview for a proposed marketplace. You can explore the browser-local demo today. No requests are sent to real professionals.

What happens to demo data?

The reference prototype saves jobs, photos, quotes, and profiles in your browser's localStorage. Other people and devices cannot see them. Use fictional contact details for testing; no accounts or payments are connected.

Are providers verified?

No. Sample profiles are fictional. New demo profiles remain review-pending. A real service would need to review identity, appropriate trade credentials, and insurance evidence before making verification claims.
